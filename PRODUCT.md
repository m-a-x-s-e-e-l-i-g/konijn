# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors of konine.art, art followers, and casual web-game players who want an immediate playful interaction without an account, tutorial commitment, or gaming expertise. The Stampkonijn route is designed for short sessions on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Konine.art presents the Konijn artwork and turns its central character into a living part of the site. The Stampkonijn game gives visitors a short score-chasing playground: aim the 3D rabbit's next bounce through an art-filled room, butt-stamp furniture, walls, floor, and ceiling, and earn the displayed euro value of every object that breaks. Success means that movement feels funny immediately, destruction escalates visibly, and players instinctively want one more run.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile, and unruly. The experience should feel handmade and affectionate even while the room is being demolished. Copy is brief, loud, and gleefully repetitive when impact lands.

## Anti-references

- Not a realistic violence simulator or a dark destruction game.
- Not a sterile 3D technology demo with generic primitives and developer-facing controls.
- Not a glossy casino, loot-box, or microtransaction experience.
- Not a conventional SaaS interface placed around a game canvas.

## Design Principles

1. Start playing before explaining. Movement and stamping must be discoverable within seconds.
2. Every impact answers back. Objects fracture, sound reacts, the room shakes, and points name the value destroyed.
3. Escalate the mess. The room should visibly remember what the rabbit has done.
4. Keep the rabbit lovable. Destruction is slapstick play directed at objects, never harm.
5. Let the artwork belong in the room. Konijn paintings and the existing site personality should make the game unmistakably part of konine.art.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and touch controls, pause and mute, readable score contrast, and a reduced-motion mode that limits camera shake and flashing while preserving gameplay. Gameplay must not depend on sound or color alone, and the game route must remain escapable with standard navigation and keyboard focus.
